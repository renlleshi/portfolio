Project 01 — Communication automation

An inbox that sorts and drafts its own replies

A workflow that reads every incoming email, decides what it is, writes a first-draft reply, and only interrupts a human when something is actually urgent.

The problem

Small teams lose real hours every week just reading and sorting email — separating a genuine emergency from a routine question, a sales lead, or spam — before anyone even starts replying.

How it works

  • Watch the inbox

    A Gmail trigger picks up every new unread message as it arrives.

  • Classify and draft with an LLM

    The email is sent to GPT-4o-mini, which returns a category (Sales, Support, Urgent, Spam), a priority, and a short suggested reply.

  • Log everything

    Every processed email is written to a Google Sheet, building a running record of what's coming in and how it was handled.

  • Draft, don't send

    For anything that isn't spam, a Gmail draft is created automatically — a person still reviews it before it goes out.

  • Escalate what matters

    If the message is flagged urgent, a Slack alert fires immediately so it doesn't sit unread in a queue.

Why it matters

The workflow doesn't try to remove the human from the loop — replies still need a review before sending. It removes the slowest, most repetitive part: reading and triaging, while leaving a clear, auditable log of every decision.
