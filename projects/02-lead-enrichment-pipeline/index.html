Project 02 — Sales & RevOps automation

Turn a raw contact list into a scored pipeline

A workflow that takes a bare lead — name, email, website — and comes back with a company summary, an industry estimate, and a fit score, without anyone opening a browser tab.

The problem

A new lead usually arrives as almost nothing — a name and a website. Before sales can decide whether it's worth a call, someone has to manually open the site, read it, and guess whether it's a good fit. That research step is where most leads quietly go cold.

How it works

  • Detect the new lead

    A trigger watches a Google Sheet and fires the moment a new row is added.

  • Pull the company's own words

    The workflow fetches the lead's website directly and strips it down to plain text.

  • Enrich and score with an LLM

    The text is summarized into a company profile — industry, estimated size — and scored 0–100 against a fit criteria.

  • Write it back automatically

    The same sheet row is updated in place — no copy-pasting between tools.

  • Surface the hot ones

    Any lead scoring 70 or above triggers an instant Slack message to the sales channel.

Why it matters

This mirrors the kind of enrichment/waterfall logic tools like Clay are built around: going from a spreadsheet of names to a researched, scored pipeline, with no manual lookup in between.
